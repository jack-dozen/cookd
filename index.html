Documentation

For Users

Welcome to CookD! Here's how to get started:

  • Input Ingredients: Type the ingredients you currently have, separated by commas.
  • Match Score: Browse recipes sorted by their match score (0-100%). The higher the score, the fewer ingredients you need to buy.
  • Price Comparison: Click on any recipe to see a breakdown of ingredient costs across Tokopedia, Alfagift, and AEON Store.
  • Save Recipes: Click the bookmark icon on any recipe to save it to your personal library.

For Developers

Want to contribute to CookD? We welcome pull requests and issues on our GitHub repository.

  • Tech Stack: Python, Flet, TinyDB, Scrapling.
  • Setup: Clone the repository, install dependencies via pip install -r requirements.txt, and run python main.py.
  • Contributing: Please read the CONTRIBUTING.md file in the repository. Fork the project, make your changes, and submit a PR to the develop branch.
  • Scrapers: If you want to add support for a new store, implement the scraper interface in the /scrapers directory.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how CookD ("we", "us", or "our") handles your data. Because CookD runs entirely locally on your machine, your privacy is our top priority.

Data Collection

We do not collect or store any personal data. The ingredients you input, the recipes you save, and your search history are all stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this information.

Third-Party Services

CookD scrapes data from third-party websites (Cookpad, Tokopedia, Alfagift, AEON Store) to provide recipe and price information. When you use these features, your IP address may be visible to these websites as per their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies if you have concerns.

Email Waitlist

If you join our waitlist, your email address is stored securely and used solely to send you updates about CookD. We will never sell or share your email with third parties.

Social Media

Currently, CookD doesn't have any official social media accounts.

We are focusing entirely on building the best possible kitchen companion. For updates, please check back on our website or join the waitlist!

If you need to contact us directly, feel free to open an issue on our GitHub repository.
